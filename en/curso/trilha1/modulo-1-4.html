MODULE 1.4

🏠 Where Hermes Lives

Terminal, local, or VPS: the possible homes for Hermes. Each has trade-offs in cost, control, and security—and knowing how to choose is what saves you headaches (and a big bill) later.

📖 The Terms: Terminal, Local, VPS

Three words before you choose your setup. Terminal is where you type commands; local is your own computer; VPS is another company's computer that you rent.

Terminal
The "black command window"
Local
Your own machine
VPS
Rented, remote computer

💡 Practical tip

VPS = "Virtual Private Server." It's like renting a room in someone else's house: convenient, but you need to lock the door.

2

🖥️ On your computer

Running it on your PC is free, easy, and secureIt works as long as your computer is on — that’s why many people use a Old MacBook, 24/7 just for that.

✓ Advantages of local

  • ✓Zero hosting costs
  • ✓Full control over your data
  • ✓Simple setup to get started

✗ Limitations

  • ✗Only runs when your PC is on
  • ✗Depends on your hardware
  • ✗Your internet connection may fluctuate

💡 Practical tip

The "old MacBook 24/7" solution gives you the best of both worlds to start: near-total uptime and zero hosting costs.

3

There are two ways: paste the command into the terminal or ask Claude Code to install it for you.

4

☁️ VPS: rent, pay, protect

A VPS runs on another company's computer (hosting). You rents, pays monthly and needs secure the ports — otherwise, there’s a risk of attack. This is the option for having Hermes running 24/7 without leaving your PC on.

📊 What to consider in a VPS

  • •Monthly cost — you pay while you use it
  • •Security — open ports are an attack surface
  • •Uptime — stays on 24/7 without depending on you
  • •Use case — e.g., rent fast inference

⚠️ Common mistake

Spin up a VPS and leave ports open without protection. On a machine exposed to the internet, that’s an invitation to intrusion. Treat security as part of setup, not as "later".

5

🔗 Be careful with recommendations

VPS providers tend to earn referral fees through affiliate linksNot every recommendation you see is neutral — there may be a commission behind it.

✓ Choose well

  • ✓Compare price, support, and region
  • ✓Read reviews from independent sources
  • ✓Be wary of “the only right one”

✗ Don't fall for

  • ✗Following only the most heavily promoted affiliate link
  • ✗Ignore costs that show up later
  • ✗Assuming recommendation = neutrality
6

🎚️ Control on your machine

Since it runs on your machine, you can ask Hermes things like "turn off the gateway" or "restart" — it manages its own infrastructure using natural language.

🛠️ Self-administration

The agent can modify its own environment because it has access to the system where it lives:

  • •"turn off the gateway" — pauses the entry point
  • •"restart" — restarts the service
  • •everything in natural language, without memorizing commands

💡 Practical tip

This self-administration power is great, but reinforces the importance of security (Track 3): an agent that controls the infrastructure needs the least access necessary.

7

🧭 Which home to choose

A quick reference for choosing between local, VPS, and cloud based on your priority.

Decision table (illustrative)

prioridade         -> escolha
custo zero/começar -> 🖥️ Local (PC, MacBook 24/7)
ligado 24/7        -> ☁️ VPS (paga mensal, proteja portas)
não quero gerir    -> 🌐 Nuvem (gerenciada, menos controle)

✓ Good practice

  • ✓Start locally; migrate only when you need 24/7 availability
  • ✓Treat security as part of your VPS setup

✗ Common mistake

  • ✗Spin up an expensive VPS before you need one
  • ✗Leave ports open without protection
